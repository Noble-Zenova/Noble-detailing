Our Methodology

How We Treat Your Vehicle

A systematic 4-phase protocol designed to protect factory materials while maximizing gloss and longevity.

PHASE 01

Decontamination

Non-acid iron fallout removal, citrus pre-wash, two-bucket hand wash, and fine clay bar decontamination to remove bonded industrial fallout.

PHASE 02

Correction & Leveling

Dual-action machine polishing with calibrated micro-abrasives to permanently remove swirls, water spots, and light scratches from the clear coat.

PHASE 03

Ceramic Shielding

Application of 9H SiO2 quartz coating that cross-links with the clear coat to create a sacrificial, ultra-hydrophobic glass barrier.
